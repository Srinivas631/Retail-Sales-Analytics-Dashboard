# Dashboard Screenshots

## Overview

This folder contains screenshots of the Power BI dashboards developed as part of the Retail Sales Analytics project.

The dashboard was designed to provide business insights related to sales performance, product profitability, customer behavior, geographical distribution, and shipping efficiency.

---

# Dashboard Pages

## 1. Executive Overview

### Purpose

Provides a high-level summary of business performance.

### KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Profit Margin %

### Visuals

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Monthly Quantity Trend
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category

### Screenshot

**Executive_Overview.png**

### Key Insights

- Sales increased steadily from 2011 to 2014.
- Chairs generated the highest revenue.
- Copiers generated the highest profit.
- Overall profit margin was approximately 14.94%.

---

## 2. Product Analytics

### Purpose

Analyzes product-level sales and profitability.

### KPIs

- Total Products
- Top Product Revenue
- Top Profit Product

### Visuals

- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Revenue Contribution by Category
- Top Categories by Profit
- Product Profitability Matrix

### Screenshot

**Product_Analytics.png**

### Key Insights

- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier generated the highest sales and profit.
- Chairs contributed the highest category revenue.
- Copiers generated the highest category profit.
- Revenue is concentrated among a small group of products.

---

## 3. Customer Analytics

### Purpose

Analyzes customer purchasing behavior and contribution to business growth.

### KPIs

- Total Customers
- Average Revenue per Customer
- Average Orders per Customer
- Average Profit per Customer

### Visuals

- Top Customers by Sales
- Top Customers by Profit
- Customer Revenue Contribution
- Customer Value Matrix

### Screenshot

**Customer_Analytics.png**

### Key Insights

- A small number of customers contribute a significant portion of revenue.
- Revenue and profit show a strong positive relationship.
- High-value customers generate substantial business impact.

---

## 4. Geography & Shipping Analytics

### Purpose

Evaluates geographical performance and shipping efficiency.

### KPIs

- Average Shipping Days
- Fastest Delivery
- Slowest Delivery
- Total States

### Visuals

- Top States by Sales
- Top States by Profit
- Geographical Sales Distribution
- Profit vs Shipping Days
- Order Volume Analysis

### Screenshot

**Geography_Shipping_Analytics.png**

### Key Insights

- California generated the highest sales and profit.
- Washington was among the strongest-performing states.
- Average delivery time was approximately 4 days.
- Shipping duration showed limited impact on profitability.
