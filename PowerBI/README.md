# Power BI Dashboard

## Overview

This folder contains the Power BI dashboard developed as part of the Retail Sales Analytics project.

The dashboard was designed to provide interactive business insights related to sales performance, product profitability, customer behavior, geographical distribution, and shipping efficiency.

---

# Dashboard Pages

## 1. Executive Overview

### Objective
Provide a high-level overview of business performance.

### KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Profit Margin %

### Visuals
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Month-wise Quantity Trend

### Business Insights
- Identified overall business growth trends.
- Monitored profitability across categories.
- Tracked sales performance over time.

---

## 2. Product Analytics

### Objective
Analyze product-level performance and profitability.

### KPIs
- Total Products
- Top Product Revenue
- Top Profit Product

### Visuals
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Revenue Contribution by Category
- Top Categories by Profit
- High Sales vs High Profit Products

### Business Insights
- Identified top-performing products.
- Analyzed category contribution to revenue.
- Compared revenue-generating and profit-generating products.

---

## 3. Customer Analytics

### Objective
Understand customer purchasing behavior and revenue contribution.

### KPIs
- Total Customers
- Average Revenue per Customer
- Average Orders per Customer
- Average Profit per Customer

### Visuals
- Top 10 Customers by Sales
- Top 10 Customers by Profit
- Customer Revenue Distribution
- Customer Value Matrix

### Business Insights
- Identified high-value customers.
- Analyzed customer profitability.
- Evaluated customer contribution to total revenue.

---

## 4. Geography & Shipping Analytics

### Objective
Analyze geographical performance and shipping efficiency.

### KPIs
- Average Shipping Days
- Fastest Delivery
- Slowest Delivery
- Total States

### Visuals
- Top States by Sales
- Top States by Profit
- Orders by Year
- Profit vs Shipping Days
- Geographical Sales Distribution Map

### Business Insights
- California generated the highest sales and profit.
- Shipping performance was evaluated across delivery durations.
- Regional sales distribution patterns were identified.

# Interactive Features

### Slicers

- State
- Category

### Cross Filtering

All visuals are interconnected and respond dynamically to user selections.

### Drill-down Analysis

Users can explore performance by:
- Product
- Customer
- State
- Category
- Year
