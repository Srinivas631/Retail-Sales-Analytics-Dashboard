# SQL Analysis

## Overview

This folder contains SQL queries and business analysis performed on the Retail Sales dataset using MySQL.

The objective of this analysis was to identify key business insights related to product performance, customer behavior, sales trends, profitability, and shipping performance.

---

## Files

### SQL_Queries.sql
Contains all SQL queries used for analysis.

### SQL_Analysis_Report.pdf / SQL_Analysis_Report.pptx
Contains SQL queries, outputs, and business insights.

---

# Business Questions & Analysis

## 1. Top 10 Products by Profit

### Objective
Identify the products generating the highest profit.

### Key Insight
Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the most profitable product.

---

## 2. Profit by State

### Objective
Determine which states contribute the highest profit.

### Key Insight
California generated the highest overall profit among all states.

---

## 3. Monthly Sales Trend

### Objective
Analyze sales performance over time.

### Key Insight
Sales showed a consistent upward trend from 2011 to 2014.

---

## 4. Top 10 Customers by Sales

### Objective
Identify customers contributing the highest revenue.

### Key Insight
A small group of customers contributed a significant portion of total sales.

---

## 5. Shipping Performance

### Objective
Evaluate order distribution across shipping durations.

### Key Insight
Most orders were delivered within a few days, indicating efficient shipping operations.

---

## 6. Profit vs Shipping Time

### Objective
Analyze the relationship between shipping duration and profitability.

### Key Insight
Profitability varied across shipping durations, highlighting operational efficiency opportunities.

---

## 7. Category-wise Profit Margin

### Objective
Compare profitability across product categories.

### Key Insight
Certain categories generated higher profit margins despite lower sales volumes.

---

## 8. Most Profitable Customers

### Objective
Identify customers contributing the highest profit.

### Key Insight
High-revenue customers were not always the most profitable customers.

---

## 9. Repeat Customers

### Objective
Identify customers with multiple orders.

### Key Insight
Repeat customers represented a valuable customer segment and contributed significantly to revenue.

---

## 10. Sales vs Profit by State

### Objective
Compare sales and profit performance across states.

### Key Insight
States with high sales did not always generate the highest profit.

---

## 11. Product Ranking by Profit

### Objective
Rank products based on total profit generated.

### Key Insight
A small number of products accounted for a large share of total profits.

---

## 12. Revenue Contribution Percentage

### Objective
Measure each category's contribution to total revenue.

### Key Insight
A few categories contributed the majority of total revenue, following the Pareto principle.
